8. Security

We apply access controls, encrypted transport (HTTPS), least-privilege API scopes and restricted internal access to protect data. 10. Revoking the TikTok connection

You can disconnect our App at any time in TikTok: open Settings and privacy → Security and login → Manage app permissions (wording may vary by region), find "9A Battery" and remove access. Removing access invalidates the tokens we hold; we then delete the stored tokens within 30 days. You can also e-mail us to request disconnection. Revoking access does not delete data already published to your TikTok account — delete that content in the TikTok app itself.

11. Cookies
